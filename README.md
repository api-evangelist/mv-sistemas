MV (MV Sistemas / MV Global Health) is a Brazilian healthcare technology company founded in 1987 in Recife, and one of Brazil's largest health-IT vendors. Its Global Health Conecta platform publishes a family of OpenAPI 3.0 integration APIs for hospitals, clinics, and health-plan operators — covering clinic scheduling and connectivity, clinical regulation, patient-care records, entry protocols, consent-based patient data sharing, events and check-in, beneficiary management, health-plan operator hubs, notifications, and facial-recognition biometrics. All APIs authenticate with an x-api-key header and require an active contract with MV Global Health.

**Areas:** Company · Healthcare · Health IT · Hospitals · Clinics · Health Insurance · FHIR · Brazil · Patient Data · Scheduling · Biometrics

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for MV sistemas: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
